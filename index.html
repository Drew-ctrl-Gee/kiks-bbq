About Us

Welcome to Kiki's BBQ Place — Ruiru's go-to spot for mouthwatering barbeque! We serve up smoky, juicy, flame-grilled goodness that keeps our customers coming back for more.

We are always open and ready to serve you at both our convenient Ruiru locations.

Whether you are grabbing a quick bite or sitting down for a full meal, we've got you covered with the best BBQ in Ruiru.
